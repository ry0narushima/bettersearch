Is the electric car market still growing?

Are electric car sales still growing worldwide, and where is the growth coming from?

The short answerYes. More than 17 million electric cars were sold in 2024, over 1 in 5 new cars worldwide 1. China made almost two thirds of those sales, while Europe stayed flat 2.

What you need to know

Sub-question 1

How fast are sales growing?

Electric car sales grew by about 25% in 2024, to more than 17 million 1. That is slower than the huge jumps of 2021 and 2022, but the market is now much bigger, so each year adds millions of cars 3.

The IEA's outlook says sales should pass 20 million in 2025. That would make about 1 in 4 new cars electric 2. Early 2025 sales were up about a third on the year before 2.

Sub-question 2

Where is the growth coming from?

China sold more than 11 million electric cars in 2024. That is more than the whole world sold just two years before 2. Almost half of all new cars sold in China were electric.

Europe stayed flat at about 3.2 million. Some subsidies ended, and EU rules on car emissions only tighten again in 2025 2. Counts for the EU alone show battery-only cars slipping a little 5.

The United States grew about 10%, to about 1.6 million. About 1 in 10 new cars sold there was electric 2.

Sub-question 3

Are electric cars getting cheaper?

Batteries are the most expensive part of an electric car. Their average price fell about 20% in 2024, to $115 per kWh 4. Cheaper batteries make cheaper cars possible.

In China, most electric cars sold in 2024 already cost less than similar petrol cars 2. In Europe and the United States, electric cars still cost more to buy on average 2.
